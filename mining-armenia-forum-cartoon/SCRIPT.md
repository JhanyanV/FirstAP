# Mining Armenia Forum — "From Resources to Opportunities"

An animated story film (~85 s, 16:9, 1080p, English) about the Mining Armenia
Forum: its history, participants and significance. All facts come from the
official site, [miningforum.am](https://miningforum.am/) (About Us, Forum 2024,
Forum 2025, Forum 2026, Speakers, Sponsors and partners).

**Style:** elegant 2D illustration in the forum's brand palette: deep indigo
`#2E3194`, midnight navy `#0D145D`, gold `#FFBA32`. Characters are generic and
stylized, with no likenesses of real people. All on-screen text is set as
typography in post, not generated.

**Production:** keyframes with Higgsfield (Nano Banana Pro), animation with
Higgsfield (Kling 3.0 Pro), narration with Higgsfield text-to-speech. The music
is an original synthesized score. The film was edited and titled with ffmpeg.

## Sources (miningforum.am)

- **About Us:** "a leading platform for dialogue, cooperation and forward-looking
  thinking on the future of Armenia's mining and metals sector"; brings together
  government, industry, finance, academia, international organizations and civil
  society.
- **Forum 2024:** September 27–28, 2024, Tsaghkadzor. Theme "Armenia's Perspectives within the
  Global Economy". 25+ speakers, 7 international guests. Tripartite memorandum
  between ZCMC, Yerevan State University and the Ministry of Territorial Administration
  and Infrastructure. Session "Education and Mining: Attracting Talent to the
  Sector".
- **Forum 2025:** October 3–4, 2025, Tsaghkadzor. 48+ speakers. Welcome speech by the
  Prime Minister. Memorandum between the Armenian Mining and Metallurgy
  Association and the Ministry of Economy. Panel "Exploration Investments and
  Beyond". Foyer exhibition of historical materials from the National Archives.
- **Forum 2026:** October 16–17, 2026, Tsaghkadzor. "From Resources to Opportunities". Held
  immediately before COP17 on Biodiversity in Armenia.
- **Home page:** 3 years · 500+ participants · 40+ speakers · 20+ countries.
  Pillars: Industry, Investment, Innovation, Sustainable Development.
- **Sponsors 2026:** strategic sponsors ZCMC and Lydian Armenia; general sponsors Ameriabank
  and Teghout.

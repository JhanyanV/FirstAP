# Open questions for AMMA

Things I left out, softened, or had to decide on while building the explainer. Every fact in the video comes from the **AMMA 2026 partner brochure, EN print edition (FINAL r2)**. Page numbers below are the brochure's own page numbers.

## Needs a decision before release

1. **Source file.** I received the brochure as a PowerPoint file (`AMMA_2026_EN_print_FINAL_r2.pptx`, 42 slides), not the PDF named in the brief. I assumed its content is identical to the PDF. Please confirm, or send the PDF if the text was changed after the PPTX export.

2. **The brief was cut off.** It ended at "5. Standards: AMMA participates in implementing TSM, Cmsi". I finished the storyline from the brochure: 5 Standards → 6 Mining Armenia Forum 2026 → 7 Next generation (universities) → 8 Closing. I also added a short "sector today" scene after the history hook. Please send the rest of the brief if you had other scenes in mind.

3. **"Participates in implementing TSM, CMSI."** The brochure (p. 24) supports something more modest: TSM *preparatory work* with member companies in cooperation with MAC in 2025 (marked "done"), and work *towards national adoption* of CMSI (consultation with ICMM in September 2026, a webinar and round table planned for October 2026). I used the brochure's wording. If AMMA members are already implementing TSM or CMSI at site level, please confirm and I will strengthen the line.

4. **Forum tense.** The video says the Forum "meets on 16–17 October … expecting over 300 participants". That is correct today (September 2026) but will be out of date once the Forum has taken place. Should there be a post-Forum version, or should this scene be made timeless?

5. **Which international partners to name.** Page 31 lists Eurometaux (memorandum, 2026), the Mining Association of Canada (TSM and industry cooperation), the Critical Minerals Association (USA; partnership and memorandum), the Canada–Armenia Chamber of Commerce and CEBA, and the Association of Mining Industrialists of Russia (memorandum and industry dialogue). For space, the video names only the first three, plus the brochure's own ">10 MoUs and international memberships" figure (p. 16). Choosing which partners to show is an editorial and diplomatic decision. Please confirm the selection.

6. **No audio in the MP4s.** Both videos are silent. `VOICEOVER.md` is timed to the picture for a recording session. Do you want me to add the recorded voice-over, music, or both once they exist? It takes one `<Audio>` line per track. The 9:16 cut has burned-in captions so it works on muted autoplay. The 16:9 cut is clean and ships with the `.srt`.

## Facts I adjusted or left out

7. **"Agarak combine from 1963."** The brochure's timeline (p. 9) says only "1963 · Agarak". It does not say "combine", so the video says "Agarak in 1963". I also used the two other dates on the same timeline: 13th c. Akhtala (first written record, by the historian Kirakos Gandzaketsi) and 1950s Kajaran.

8. **Inconsistent Forum numbers on p. 32.** "Over three years: 500+ participants, 40+ speakers, 20+ countries" conflicts with the per-year figures on the same page (2024: 25+ speakers; 2025: 48+ speakers, which already makes 73+). I used only the 2026 expectations from p. 33 (300+ participants, 20+ countries, ~50 speakers). You may also want to fix p. 32 in the brochure.

9. **Estimates left out on purpose.** I didn't use these forward-looking or "Association's estimate" figures: ~20% GDP potential and up to USD 1 bn a year to the budget (p. 3), USD 3 bn export potential and ≈5% of state tax revenues (p. 8). I also left out the market prices on p. 5, which date quickly. Tell me if any of these should go in.

10. **No map of Armenia.** I deliberately didn't draw a map of Armenia or any country borders, to avoid accuracy and border-sensitivity problems in a film for governments and partners. The international scene uses an abstract longitude/latitude grid with city dots. If you want the operating-mines map from p. 10, please supply a verified vector map.

11. **Advocacy is shown as proposals, not wins.** Pages 27–28 mostly record proposals as *submitted or discussed*, without adoption outcomes. So the checklist uses a neutral document icon, not tick marks, and the voice-over says "proposals on…". The one stated outcome (the GPS requirement narrowed to certain floodplain deposits) isn't in the video. Should it be?

12. **CRIRSCO is shown as a goal.** The Standards scene flips a card from "Soviet reserve classification" to "CRIRSCO-aligned reporting", labelled "THE GOAL". It is not shown as done, because pp. 22–23 describe a government working group and a phased transition that is still under way.

## Brand and language

13. **Logo quality.** The gold triangle logo is the raster PNG embedded in the brochure (467 × 417 px). Its "AMMA" lettering is a transparent knock-out, so on navy it shows as navy, as on the brochure cover. For the sharpest result, please send the vector logo (SVG, AI or EPS). The older round "Union" logo (p. 13, used until 2025) is not used anywhere.

14. **"ՀՀՄԱ".** The brief mentions the Armenian abbreviation ՀՀՄԱ, but the English brochure doesn't contain it. On screen I used the full Armenian name from p. 42 instead ("Հայաստանի հանքարդյունաբերության և մետալուրգիայի ասոցիացիա"). Should ՀՀՄԱ be added next to "AMMA"?

15. **Pronunciation.** The pronunciation notes in `VOICEOVER.md` (Akhtala, Alaverdi, Kajaran, Agarak, Tsaghkadzor, Eurometaux) are my suggestions. Please have them checked before recording.

16. **Brochure typo (FYI).** The intro on p. 24 reads "AMMA Canadian TSM or the newly developed consolidated standard (CMSI) are the best choices." A verb seems to be missing, e.g. "AMMA considers…".
